# Ideas

## Go to next meeting

Add a **Next meeting** button to the HUD that finds the next calendar meeting and opens its join link, such as a Microsoft Teams meeting link.

Show the meeting title and start time so it is clear which meeting the button will open. This would provide a quick shortcut without navigating the Teams calendar or chats.

The button could live in the web HUD or in a native WinForms panel alongside WebView2.
